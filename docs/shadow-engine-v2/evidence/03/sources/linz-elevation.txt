Access elevation data
Digital elevation models and point clouds are free to download or stream as Open Data. The data can also be previewed as 3D hillshade visualisation through the LINZ Data Service and LINZ Basemaps.
The merged national products below are automatically updated as newer data becomes available.
LINZ Data Service
LiDAR-based elevation models, point clouds, and hillshades:
National 1m Digital Elevation Model (DEM)
National 1m Digital Surface Model (DSM)
National 1m DEM Hillshade
National 1m DSM Hillshade
Index of individual datasets
Registry of Open Data on Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Bulk data as Cloud Optimised GeoTIFFs:
New Zealand Elevation dataset
LINZ Basemaps
Elevation layer visualisation and streaming services:
Basemaps.linz.govt.nz
