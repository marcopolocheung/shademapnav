Products that are no longer disseminated on the CLMS website
EU-DEM
EU-DEM is not maintained anymore and is no longer available on our website.
We recommend users to check the Copernicus DEM product available at 30 m spatial resolution.
Please read more
here
.
European Settlement Map (ESM)
European Settlement Map is no longer available on our website.
The product is now part of the Exposure Mapping, which is a new component of the  Copernicus Emergency Management Service.
Please read more
here
.
Daily pan-European Vegetation Properties
Daily pan-European Fraction of Absorbed Photosynthetically Active Radiation and Leaf Area Index are not maintained anymore and are no longer available on our website.
We recommend users to explore 10-daily global
Vegetation Properties
.
Daily pan-European Vegetation Indices
Daily pan-European Normalised Difference Vegetation Index, Plant Phenology Index and Vegetation Indices Quality Flag are not maintained anymore and are no longer available on our website.
We recommend users to explore 10-daily global
Vegetation Indices
.
